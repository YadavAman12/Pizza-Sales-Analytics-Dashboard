# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built using the Pizza Sales dataset. It provides business insights into revenue, sales trends, customer ordering behavior, and product performance.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Pages

### 🏠 Page 1 – Executive Dashboard

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Pizzas Sold
- Average Order Value
- Daily Order Trend
- Revenue by Day
- Sales Share by Size
- Sales Share by Category

---

### 📊 Page 2 – Sales Analysis

- Hourly Trend of Orders
- Pizzas Sold by Category
- Sales Share by Size
- Sales Share by Category

---

### 🏆 Page 3 – Top & Bottom Performers

- Top 5 Pizzas by Revenue
- Bottom 5 Pizzas by Revenue
- Top 5 Pizzas by Quantity
- Bottom 5 Pizzas by Quantity

---

### 📋 Page 4 – Business Insights

- Peak Sales Timing
- Sales Performance
- Best Selling Pizza
- Data Source Information

---

- Peak ordering time occurs during lunch and dinner hours.
- Top-selling pizzas identified through revenue and quantity analysis.
